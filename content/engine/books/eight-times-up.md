## Story in short
Yuki, a young mountain goat in the snowy mountains of Japan, dreams of climbing Cloud Rock to watch the sunrise like the big goats. Her friend Taro, a snow monkey, has a little red roly-poly toy that always rocks back upright. On her first try Yuki slips on a shiny rock and tumbles into soft snow, and she wonders if she just isn't a climber. Seeing the toy pop up, she notices what the fall taught her and tries again. By sunset she has fallen four times; she rests, then counts what each fall taught her. Day after day she practises, a little higher each time. She meets Hina, a ptarmigan chick crying after a fall, and shares her lessons. On the big climb a gust of wind sends her sliding down: the seventh and biggest fall. Hearing the toy pop up, she rises an eighth time, uses everything she learned, and reaches Cloud Rock above a sea of clouds at sunrise. By spring the little ones climb too, and Yuki still falls sometimes, and laughs.

## Characters
- **Yuki** (Japanese serow, called a "mountain goat" in the text): fluffy grey coat, short dark horns, big ears, a white star-shaped patch on her forehead. Determined, honest about her feelings.
- **Taro** (Japanese macaque / snow monkey): pink face, thick brown-grey fur, always carries a round red roly-poly toy; loves hot springs.
- **Hina** (rock ptarmigan chick): white winter feathers, one tiny brown feather on her head.
- **The big goats:** adult serows seen from a distance; they never coach or praise Yuki.

## The real animals
- **Japanese serow** (*Capricornis crispus*): a goat-antelope found only in Japan's mountain forests (Honshu, Shikoku, Kyushu); a protected "special natural monument" in Japan. Usually solitary. (It's not a true goat; the text simplifies.)
- **Japanese macaque** (*Macaca fuscata*): the most northerly-living non-human primate; famous groups at Jigokudani bathe in hot springs in winter.
- **Rock ptarmigan** (*Lagopus muta*): grouse that turns white in winter; in Japan it lives only in the high mountains of central Honshu (Japanese Alps) and is endangered there (check status wording).
- The roly-poly toy echoes real Japanese self-righting dolls: the Daruma and the *okiagari-koboshi* of Aizu.

## Setting and environment
Snowy mountains of central Japan from deep winter to spring: beech forest, steaming hot spring, rocky slopes, ridges, a snowfield, and Cloud Rock above a sea of clouds. Ends with snow melting and the first purple flowers.

## The value
For a child: every fall teaches you something, and getting up again is your choice. For grown-ups: resilience without toughness-worship: falls are information, not verdicts; resting is allowed; nobody is hurt (soft snow); she shares what she learns; she still falls at the end. Avoids "never give up even when it hurts", "practice makes perfect", proving yourself to others, and the friend fixing it.

## Where the value comes from
Japanese proverb *nana korobi ya oki* (七転び八起き), "fall down seven times, get up eight", long linked with self-righting folk toys (Daruma; the Aizu *okiagari-koboshi*, made for centuries). ## Questions to ask

2. What did each fall teach her?
3. Why do you think she rested before trying again?
4. How did Yuki help Hina?
